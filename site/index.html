What I Can Take Off Your Plate

Inbox & Enquiry Triage

Typically one week

Turn a shared inbox from a pit into a process.

  • Audit of what arrives, how often, and what genuinely needs a human
  • Categorisation rules and label structure
  • Response templates for the recurring 80%
  • Escalation paths — what reaches you, what doesn't
  • Agreed response times

You end up with a working triage system and a set of templates, in your own accounts.

Handover & Continuity Pack

Typically two weeks

What happens when the person who knows everything leaves.

  • Written procedures for every recurring process
  • Access and credentials register
  • Supplier, venue and contact list with relationship notes
  • Calendar of annual obligations — renewals, reports, deadlines, insurance
  • Induction pack for whoever comes next

Everything is written down, so the next hire becomes survivable.

Event Production Pack

Typically two to three weeks

Stop rebuilding every concert from scratch.

  • Your last event reverse-engineered into a reusable master timeline
  • Supplier, venue and technical checklists
  • Run sheet and on-the-day schedule templates
  • Communication sequences — artists, crew, audience
  • Contingency list and post-event wash-up template
  • One live event run alongside you, then refined

The pack is yours to use for every event afterwards.

Season Production System

Typically three to four weeks

Every event, contract, deadline, supplier and budget line for a whole season, in one place.

  • Central build: events, dates, venues, artists, contracts, budgets, deadlines
  • Deadline reminders set up and running
  • Views by event, by month, by status, by owner
  • Populated with your current or upcoming season
  • A session with your team so they can run it without me

The system stays in your accounts, with a full season already in it.

Ongoing support

One to three days a week

Most arrangements run one to three days a week. Some are a standing weekly rhythm, some are a single event or one busy season.

  • Inbox and diary managed daily, to an agreed response time
  • First point of contact for artists, venues and suppliers
  • Events run end to end — timelines, logistics, on-site coordination
  • Systems kept current as the season changes
  • Budgets tracked, invoices prepared, records kept clean

We can start small and adjust once we both know what the work actually takes.
