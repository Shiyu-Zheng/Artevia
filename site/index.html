Before After

I came to operations from the stage.

Hi, I'm Alina — a classical pianist, an educator, and an arts coordinator. A decade in this industry, on stage, backstage, and behind the desk.

I've been on both sides of the stage, so I know what a musician needs before they ask — and what quietly falls apart in a programme when nobody is holding it.

Operations is care. The best work comes from people who trust and support each other, just like an ensemble.

ArteVia comes from the Latin: a path made through art. It was mine, and I'd like it to be one for other people too.
