About

I came to operations from the stage.

Alina Zheng

Hi, I'm Alina — a classical pianist, an educator, and an arts coordinator. A decade in this industry, on stage, backstage, and behind the desk.

From liaising with artists and partners to running competitions and concerts: securing venues, arranging instrument hire and staging, building the schedules, and keeping the invoicing and records straight underneath it all.

I've been on both sides of the stage, so I know what an artist needs before they ask — and what quietly falls apart in a programme when nobody is holding it.

Operations is care. The best work comes from people who trust and support each other, just like an ensemble.

ArteVia comes from the Latin: a path made through art. It was mine, and I'd like it to be one for other people too.

How I like to work

As a minimalist, I believe less is more, so I approach systems and workflows the same way.

The best system isn't the one with the most features, or the one that looks most impressive in a demo. It's the one with every unnecessary step taken out, so it takes almost no effort to use. Tools are supposed to serve the people using them.

I take my cues from nature. Build an ecosystem properly — in work or in life — and it feeds itself: nothing needs forcing, and everything stays in balance.
