Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who I am

This site is operated by Shiyu Zheng, known professionally as Alina Zheng, an independent arts operations practitioner based in the United Kingdom, trading as ArteVia. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact hello@arte-via.uk.

What I collect

I only collect information you choose to give me:

  • Your name and email address, when you send an enquiry or email me directly.
  • Any details you include about your work, your organisation or the support you're looking for.
  • Basic scheduling information, if you book a call.

I do not use advertising trackers, and I do not build profiles of visitors.

Why I use it

To reply to you, to discuss whether I'm the right fit, and — if we go on to work together — to carry out the work we've agreed. Nothing else. I will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

Legal basis

Where you contact me about working together, I process your information on the basis of legitimate interests and, once engaged, to perform our contract. Where you consent to something specific, that consent is the basis, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who else sees it

Only the services I use to run this business, and only as far as necessary:

  • Zoho Mail — for receiving and sending email.
  • Formspree — for delivering the enquiry form on this site.
  • Calendly — if you book a call.
  • GitHub Pages — for serving this site.

I do not sell your information, and I do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Where your information goes

Formspree, Calendly and GitHub are United States companies, so sending the form, booking a call or simply loading this page involves a transfer of information outside the UK. Each of them has safeguards in place for those transfers. My email is hosted in the EU, so anything you send me directly stays within the UK and EU. If you would rather not use a service that involves a transfer, email me instead of using the booking link.

Confidentiality in client work

If we work together, I will handle your correspondence, contracts, artist relationships and financial records. All of it stays confidential, during the engagement and afterwards. I do not discuss client business publicly, and I do not use client names as references without asking first.

Keeping it secure

Your information sits on password-protected, encrypted devices and in accounts with two-factor authentication switched on wherever it is offered. No system is completely secure and I won't pretend otherwise — so the other half of the answer is that I hold as little as possible, and delete what I no longer need.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within twelve months. Records relating to actual client work are kept for six years, which is what HMRC requires for tax and accounting, and then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email hello@arte-via.uk and I'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. It also makes no third-party requests at all — the fonts, styles and scripts are served from this site rather than from someone else's network, so simply reading a page tells no one but the host that you were here. The enquiry form is plain HTML with no third-party script behind it, so nothing reaches Formspree until you press send. If you follow the scheduling link, that service may set its own cookies once you arrive; see its policy for details.

Links to other sites

Where this site links elsewhere — a scheduling tool, an organisation I've worked with — those sites have their own policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
